- [#40](https://github.com/openfilm/openfilm/pull/40) [`be758b9`](https://github.com/openfilm/openfilm/commit/be758b9fe010d03bca33d250617e3e52f49d77f3) Thanks [@PazChen](https://github.com/PazChen)! - Desktop: an agent's own notices show apart from its reply. - [#46](https://github.com/openfilm/openfilm/pull/46) [`e4e67fe`](https://github.com/openfilm/openfilm/commit/e4e67fe860048be1df2c6135c805cc773d85ca98) Thanks [@PazChen](https://github.com/PazChen)! - Your own key: each model has the reasoning efforts you give it.
